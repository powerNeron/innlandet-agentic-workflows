## Proposed scalable authorization; NOT ACTIVE

The previous exact URL/paragraph approval remains authoritative until the owner
explicitly approves a new, independently reviewed processing policy. Merely
changing `reviewed_only`, trusting an official hostname, receiving HTTP 200 or
passing an automated text filter is insufficient. No code change here issues
new fact grants. The injectable `DiscoveryProfile` authorizes only bounded
transient candidate discovery after owner/independent review, never general

A future processing profile should record:

1. Exact authority/municipality, host and permitted discovery mechanisms, explicit
   effective/expiry dates, owner decision and independent review hashes.
2. Distinct permissions for transient acquisition, candidate review, retained
   non-personal evidence, reuse and optional external-model transfer. Official
   domain approval alone is never evidence or OpenAI consent.
3. Version/hash of each MIME parser and admission rule. Preserve whole
   article/group/heading/row/footnote context, dates, jurisdiction and evidence
   scope. PDF/Office stay unsupported until bounded adversarial and actual
   extraction tests exist. Unknown/ambiguous context refuses, without truncating
   conditions or inventing completeness.
4. A separately reviewed, non-personal query-admission process for external
   search. Raw user text, names, addresses or clinical details must not leave
   simply because an NLP classifier calls them municipal. Without a safe query
   authorization, navigation and local relevance matching are the only options.
5. Pinned public-IP HTTPS, DNS/SSRF, TLS, redirect, robots/noindex, expiry/revocation
   and kill-switch checks on every request. No linked host becomes authorized
   by association. Request/depth/candidate/time/byte/rate limits apply jointly to
   discovery, provider search, documents and fallback; no uncontrolled crawl.
6. Per-request receipts binding URL/body/parser/profile/excerpt hashes, actual
   retrieval time, separate publisher/update and applicability dates. A fresh
   observation does not prove a current price or complete list. Keep unreviewed
   paths/labels/raw HTML and personal data out of logs and model input.
7. An explicit public-authority scope for national guidance and separate review
   for other supplements. Neither can attest a municipality's own conditions.
   Recheck transfer rights immediately before and after fetch/model/publication.
