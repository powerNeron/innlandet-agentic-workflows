# Limitations and reflection

The sample is small, repeated and selected during development/evaluation. There is no blind human relevance or truth assessment. Exact paragraph support is not semantic fact checking; approved text may be outdated or have conditions in other paragraphs. Approval expires on 2026-10-28 and was not changed.

A global emergency stop and aggregate token/cost budget have not been demonstrated as application controls. The GPU stop exists only in the experiment tool, sampled between waves, and first observed 80 °C after the specified 78 °C threshold had been crossed. No new calls were submitted after that observation; the plan was not completed. The small concurrency group does not establish a production SLA.

Human survey responses are absent. Viewports are emulated in Chrome; no physical mobile or screen-reader study was performed. The complete final assignment and original template are unavailable. This is not evidence of full assignment compliance or complete safety. Historical failed injection/load experiments must remain visible rather than being hidden behind current passing tests.
